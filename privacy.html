Privacy Policy

This page explains what information MindThinkMedia may collect, why we may collect it, and how third-party services may be involved when you use this website.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

About this policy

MindThinkMedia respects the privacy of its visitors. We aim to collect only the information that is reasonably needed to operate the website, communicate with readers and improve the experience of using the site.

By using MindThinkMedia, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information you provide

You can read MindThinkMedia without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

However, if you use our contact form, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you send to us

We use this information only for purposes such as reading your message, responding when appropriate and handling feedback or questions.

Automatically collected information

Like many websites, some technical information may be processed automatically when you visit MindThinkMedia. This can include information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location based on internet connection information, referral pages or general website usage data.

This kind of information may be processed by our hosting, analytics or other website service providers.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services can place on a user's device to remember information or understand how a website is used.

MindThinkMedia itself may use necessary website technologies, and third-party services used by the site may also use cookies or similar technologies.

You can usually control or delete cookies using your browser settings.

Advertising and Google AdSense

MindThinkMedia may use advertising services such as Google AdSense in the future.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements according to applicable settings, policies and legal requirements.

Google's advertising technologies may use information about visits to this website and other websites for advertising and measurement purposes.

Visitors may be able to manage advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings and through their own browser or device privacy controls.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use MindThinkMedia, such as which articles are visited and how the website performs.

Analytics information is generally used to understand overall website activity and improve the reader experience rather than to personally identify individual visitors.

Third-party services

MindThinkMedia relies on third-party services to provide certain website functions.

These may include website hosting services such as Netlify, contact-form or email delivery services such as EmailJS, analytics providers and, if enabled in the future, advertising providers such as Google.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

External links

Articles on MindThinkMedia may contain links to other websites, research papers, products, services or other online resources.

Once you leave MindThinkMedia, the privacy practices of the external website apply. We are not responsible for how another website collects or uses information.

Affiliate links

MindThinkMedia may use affiliate links in some future articles or recommendations.

If affiliate links are used, clicking one may allow the relevant affiliate platform or merchant to use cookies or other tracking technologies to identify that a visit originated from MindThinkMedia.

Relevant affiliate relationships will be disclosed where appropriate.

How we protect information

We take reasonable steps to operate MindThinkMedia responsibly and limit unnecessary collection of personal information.

However, no website, internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

MindThinkMedia is a general-audience informational website. We do not intentionally ask children to provide sensitive personal information.

If you believe personal information belonging to a child has been submitted through the website inappropriately, you can contact us so that we can review the situation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services we use, advertising practices or legal requirements change.

When significant changes are made, the updated date at the top of this page will be changed.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how MindThinkMedia handles information, you can contact us using the contact form on the website.
